TallyTV Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

1. Overview

TallyTV is a media tracking app provided by onelldev. TallyTV does not require a separate TallyTV account, and onelldev does not operate a TallyTV account backend that receives your media library, watch history, or sync credentials. The app communicates directly with services you choose to connect and with the advertising and purchase providers described below.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

  • Library data: Watchlists, watched history, episode progress, media details, sync history, app settings, and reward counters are stored locally.
  • Connection credentials: SIMKL and Trakt access tokens and the Yamtrack password are stored in the Apple Keychain. The Yamtrack server address and username are stored in app settings.
  • Purchase status: TallyTV stores and reads entitlement status needed to provide purchased features.

3. Media and Sync Services

Connecting a service is optional. When you connect or sync, TallyTV sends and receives media identifiers, list status, watched history, and episode progress as needed:

  • SIMKL and Trakt: Data is exchanged directly with the account you authorize.
  • Yamtrack: Data and login credentials are sent directly to the Yamtrack server address you configure. Yamtrack is self-hosted, so that server's operator controls its data and retention. If you choose an unencrypted HTTP server, traffic is not protected by HTTPS.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB): TallyTV sends requests such as media identifiers and search queries to retrieve titles, artwork, cast, release information, and related metadata.

Data already synced to a connected service is governed by that service and is not deleted when you remove local TallyTV data.

4. Advertising and Consent

The free version uses Google AdMob for banner ads and optional rewarded ads. Google and its partners may process device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics, and consent choices. The advertising identifier is available only when permitted by your device settings and Apple's tracking authorization.

TallyTV uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and store advertising consent where required. When available, you can review advertising choices under Settings > Privacy Choices. Rewarded ads are optional and are used only when you choose to receive a temporary ad-free period or an extra manual sync.

5. Purchases

Apple processes subscriptions and one-time purchases. TallyTV reads signed transaction and entitlement information, including the product purchased and whether it is active, expired, or revoked. onelldev does not receive your payment-card details. Apple and App Store Connect may provide transaction and sales reporting under Apple's policies.

6. Support Communications

TallyTV does not create a developer-held user profile. If you contact support, onelldev receives your email address and the information you include. Support messages are used to respond to you, troubleshoot issues, and maintain reasonable support records.

7. Sharing and Third-Party Policies

onelldev does not sell your personal information. Data is shared only as needed with services you choose to use, with Google for advertising, with Apple for purchases, or when required for legal, fraud-prevention, or security purposes.

8. Retention and Deletion

  • Local library data remains on your device until you delete it. Reset All Data removes the local library, episode progress, sync history, and cache, but it does not delete data already synced to another service.
  • Disconnect SIMKL, Trakt, or Yamtrack in TallyTV to remove that service's locally stored connection credentials. For certainty, disconnect services before uninstalling because Keychain items can persist depending on Apple device and restore behavior.
  • To delete data held by SIMKL, Trakt, Yamtrack, TMDB, Google, or Apple, use that provider's controls or contact its operator. Their retention rules apply independently.
  • You may ask onelldev to delete a support conversation, subject to legal and security recordkeeping requirements.

9. Security

TallyTV uses the Apple Keychain for sync secrets and HTTPS for SIMKL, Trakt, TMDB, Google, and Apple communications. Yamtrack security depends on the server address you configure. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated as TallyTV changes. The latest version and effective date will remain available on this page.
