Yamtrack Companion Support

Help with connections, offline sync, notifications, ads, and purchases.

Connection help

  • Confirm the Yamtrack website opens from the same iPhone or iPad.
  • Use HTTPS for a public server. Local IP addresses and .local hosts may use HTTP.
  • Include the correct port and any path used by your reverse proxy.
  • If username/password login is unavailable, use the SSO option.

Offline changes

Open Settings > Offline Sync Center to review, retry, or discard queued changes. Do not disconnect until pending items have synchronized or been reviewed, because disconnecting deliberately clears that account's local queue.

Ads and purchases

Open Settings > Ad privacy choices where available to revisit consent. Open Settings > Remove Ads to purchase or restore the permanent ad-free entitlement with your Apple ID.

Contact support

Include the app version, iOS version, device model, Yamtrack server version, affected screen, and exact error message. Never send a password, session cookie, webhook credential, or CSRF token.
