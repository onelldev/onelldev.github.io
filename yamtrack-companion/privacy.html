Yamtrack Companion Privacy Policy

A native, independently developed companion for self-hosted Yamtrack servers.

Effective August 7, 2026

1. Your Yamtrack account

Yamtrack Companion connects directly to the self-hosted Yamtrack server address you select. Your username, password, session cookies, media activity, and server responses are not sent to the app developer. Your password is used only to sign in and is never stored. Session cookies are stored in the iOS Keychain.

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores cached artwork, home, library, calendar and statistics data, preferences, and queued offline changes on your device. Disconnecting removes the saved session, account cache, downloaded artwork, and queued changes from that device. Data held by your Yamtrack server remains under the server owner's control.

3. Advertising and consent

Unless you purchase Remove Ads, Google Mobile Ads and its partners may process IP address, coarse location, device identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, performance information, diagnostics, and consent choices to deliver and measure advertising. Yamtrack Companion disables Google's publisher first-party identifier and uses Google's User Messaging Platform for required privacy choices.

You can revisit ad privacy choices from the app's Settings screen when required. Read the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is processed by Apple through StoreKit. Yamtrack Companion receives only the verified entitlement needed to decide whether ads should be shown. The developer does not receive or store your payment-card information. Read the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Notifications and SSO

Release alerts are scheduled locally on your device from Yamtrack calendar information after you opt in. SSO is the only feature that presents web content and is used solely to authenticate with your Yamtrack server and identity provider.

6. Control, retention, and security

You can disable release alerts, withdraw ad consent where applicable, restore purchases, and disconnect from Yamtrack at any time. HTTPS is required for public Yamtrack addresses. Plain HTTP is accepted only for local-network servers and is not encrypted, so people with access to that network path may be able to read the traffic.

To delete data stored by Yamtrack, contact the operator of your self-hosted server. To request deletion of a support conversation, email onellromandev@gmail.com, subject to reasonable legal and security recordkeeping requirements.

7. Independent companion

Yamtrack Companion is an independently developed, unofficial client for self-hosted Yamtrack servers. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Yamtrack project or its maintainers.

8. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to onellromandev@gmail.com. For troubleshooting, visit Yamtrack Companion Support.
