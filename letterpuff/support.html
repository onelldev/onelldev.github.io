A little help for grown-ups

Let's keep the discoveries growing.

Questions about Letterpuff, a purchase or your saved garden? Contact onelldev for help.

Email support
onellromandev@gmail.com

Tell us your app version, device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and what happened. A screenshot can help, but please remove personal details. Never send passwords, payment-card information or your child's personal information.

What is free?

Letters, Letter Fit, Numbers and My Garden are free. Optional Premium adds Listen, Pictures, Trace, Match, Missing Letter, Memory and Play Remix. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. The purchase screen shows Apple's price for your region.

Open the Grown-up Corner

Tap the settings icon at the top right of Home, then answer the displayed grown-up question. Here you can adjust spoken prompts, gentle taps, animation and difficulty. Purchase and restore controls are behind the grown-up check.

Restore Premium

Open the Grown-up Corner, choose Explore Full Unlock, then Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Opening the purchase information screen does not buy anything; Apple asks for confirmation when making a purchase.

If a purchase is pending approval, free play remains available while Apple processes it. For billing or refund requests, use Apple's purchase support. If restoration still does not work, contact us with the message shown in the app. Do not repurchase just to troubleshoot.

Start with easier games

The learning restart screen explains the changes before you confirm. A learning restart can keep the earned garden. Restarting the garden is a separate choice. Comfort settings and purchase ownership are not reset. You can cancel without changing progress.

Use progress on another device

Where iCloud saves are available in your app version, a grown-up can turn them on in the Grown-up Corner. Use the same iCloud Apple Account on both devices. No separate Letterpuff account is needed. iCloud saves are free; purchases restore separately through Apple.

If the app says Waiting to sync, it has local changes waiting for iCloud. Check the connection and available iCloud storage, then try syncing again from the grown-up area. If it reports an account change or separate earlier saves, follow the explanation in the app before choosing which save to continue. If iCloud saves are unavailable in your build, progress continues to save on that device.

Turning sync off does not delete your local progress or the last cloud copy. Please contact support before deleting the app while investigating missing progress. See the privacy policy for storage and removal details.

Sound and motion

Check spoken prompts and gentle taps in the Grown-up Corner, along with the device's volume and audio output. Reduced animation can be selected in the app; Letterpuff also respects the device's Reduce Motion setting for supported effects.

Privacy questions

Letterpuff has no ads or analytics SDKs. Local saves, optional private iCloud saves, Apple purchases and support messages are explained in our privacy policy. Send questions to onellromandev@gmail.com.
