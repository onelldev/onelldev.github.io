A little help for grown-ups

Let's keep the discoveries growing.

Questions about Letterpuff, a purchase or your saved garden? Contact onelldev for help.

Email support
onellromandev@gmail.com

Tell us your app version, device model, iOS, iPadOS or Android version, and what happened. A screenshot can help, but please remove personal details. Never send passwords, payment-card information or your child's personal information.

iPhone and iPad: free games and purchases

Letters, Letter Fit, Numbers and My Garden are free. Optional Premium adds Listen, Pictures, Trace, Match, Missing Letter, Memory and Play Remix. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. The purchase screen shows Apple's price for your region.

Open the Grown-up Corner

Tap the settings icon at the top right of Home, then answer the displayed grown-up question. Here you can adjust spoken prompts, gentle taps, animation and difficulty. Purchase and restore controls are behind the grown-up check.

iPhone and iPad: restore Premium

Open the Grown-up Corner, choose Explore Full Unlock, then Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Opening the purchase information screen does not buy anything; Apple asks for confirmation when making a purchase.

If a purchase is pending approval, free play remains available while Apple processes it. For billing or refund requests, use Apple's purchase support. If restoration still does not work, contact us with the message shown in the app. Do not repurchase just to troubleshoot.

Start with easier games

The learning restart screen explains the changes before you confirm. A learning restart can keep the earned garden. Restarting the garden is a separate choice. Comfort settings and purchase ownership are not reset. You can cancel without changing progress.

iPhone and iPad: use progress on another device

Where iCloud saves are available in your app version, a grown-up can turn them on in the Grown-up Corner. Use the same iCloud Apple Account on both devices. No separate Letterpuff account is needed. iCloud saves are free; purchases restore separately through Apple.

If the app says Waiting to sync, it has local changes waiting for iCloud. Check the connection and available iCloud storage, then try syncing again from the grown-up area. If it reports an account change or separate earlier saves, follow the explanation in the app before choosing which save to continue. If iCloud saves are unavailable in your build, progress continues to save on that device.

Turning sync off does not delete your local progress or the last cloud copy. Please contact support before deleting the app while investigating missing progress. See the privacy policy for storage and removal details.

Android: free games and Full Unlock

Letters, Letter Fit, Number Hunt and My Garden are free. Optional Full Unlock adds Letter Hunt, Picture Patch, Trace & Sparkle, Letter Buddies, Missing Letter, Memory Garden and Play Remix. It is one purchase with no subscription. Google Play shows your regional price.

Android: restore a purchase

Open the Grown-up Corner, choose Explore Full Unlock, then Restore Purchases. Use the Google Play account that purchased Full Unlock. Opening the information screen does not make a purchase. If a purchase is pending, free activities remain available. Do not repurchase to troubleshoot. Contact support with the exact message if restoration fails.

On a new or reinstalled copy, opening the Grown-up Corner starts purchase checks. Restoring a garden save alone does not start them. After that first setup, Letterpuff can recheck purchase status on returning to the foreground. Buying and manually restoring still require the grown-up area.

If purchase-recovery setup could not be saved, try Restore Purchases again from the Grown-up Corner. If the message persists, contact support. Do not delete the app or clear storage to troubleshoot it.

Android: keep progress when reinstalling

Android can back up committed learning progress, your garden, saved rounds and comfort settings. On supported Google devices, check the backup account and completed app backup under Android Settings → Google → Backup. Menu names vary by device. Use the same Google Account for restoration. Letterpuff does not read your account identity or verify whether a backup completed.

An eligible completed backup can restore during reinstallation or device setup before Letterpuff opens. Backups are periodic, so recent play may be missing. Android determines eligibility; restoration is not guaranteed. Check backup details before removing the app. A backup from a newer Android version may not restore to an older one.

There is no in-app restore-now control or live merge between two active Android devices. iPhone saves do not transfer to Android. Google Play purchase restoration is separate. The gameplay backup excludes purchase ownership, the purchase-setup reminder, temporary files and speech assets.

Turning Google backup off can delete device-wide backups, including other apps’ recovery data. Google also documents deletion after 57 days of device inactivity. Do not toggle backup off or delete a device backup merely to troubleshoot Letterpuff. Review Google’s backup and restore instructions and backup retention information.

Android: sound and motion

Spoken prompts use bundled audio and on-device speech; the microphone is not recorded. Check speech and music in the Grown-up Corner, device volume and selected audio output. A suitable installed offline English Android voice can provide fallback speech. If a prompt is unavailable, report the exact line, device and app version. Speech and music can be switched off for quiet play.

iPhone and iPad: sound and motion

Check spoken prompts and gentle taps in the Grown-up Corner, along with the device's volume and audio output. Reduced animation can be selected in the app; Letterpuff also respects the device's Reduce Motion setting for supported effects.

Privacy questions

Letterpuff has no ads and does not use learning analytics. Local saves, platform backup, Apple and Google Play purchases, and support messages are explained in our privacy policy. Send questions to onellromandev@gmail.com.
