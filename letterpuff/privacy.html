Privacy, explained simply.

Letterpuff is a learning game provided by onelldev. There are no ads, analytics SDKs or separate Letterpuff accounts.

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Progress saves on your device. Where available, a grown-up can choose free private iCloud saves. Apple handles purchases. If you email support, we receive the message you choose to send.

Information on your device

Letterpuff stores learning progress, completed activities, difficulty, earned rewards, garden placements and feeding history locally. It also stores preferences such as speech, haptics and reduced animation. This information lets the game remember where you left off.

The app does not ask for a child's name, date of birth, email address, photos, contacts or location. It does not record the microphone. Spoken prompts use audio and speech processing on the device. We do not run a Letterpuff server that receives gameplay progress.

Optional private iCloud saves

In versions where iCloud saves are available, a grown-up can turn them on in the Grown-up Corner. The app then sends learning progress, earned rewards, garden changes, feeding records and save identifiers to a private CloudKit database associated with the device's iCloud Apple Account. Save identifiers help combine changes and avoid duplicate rewards. These saves are not public and are not shared with other players.

Both devices must use the same iCloud Apple Account to share progress. Family Sharing alone does not share a save between different accounts. Comfort settings and an unfinished Letter Fit round stay on each device; purchases are handled separately through Apple. The app keeps a local iCloud account reference to detect account changes and avoid mixing saves.

Local saving continues offline. Sync depends on iCloud availability, connectivity and account storage. Apple operates iCloud and processes account and service information under Apple's Privacy Policy. Availability of iCloud saves can vary by app version.

Purchases through Apple

Letters, Letter Fit, Numbers and My Garden are free. Optional Premium is a one-time purchase for six additional games and Play Remix, with no subscription. Apple processes payment and restoration through StoreKit. Letterpuff reads verified transaction and purchase-status information to provide access. We do not receive your payment-card details, and purchase ownership is not stored in the gameplay cloud save.

Apple may provide the developer with App Store sales and transaction reports under its own service policies. Letterpuff does not add advertising or analytics tracking to purchases.

Support messages and this website

If you contact onellromandev@gmail.com, we receive your email address and anything you include. We use that information to answer your request and investigate the issue. Please avoid sending a child's personal details, passwords, payment-card information or private Apple Account information. Support email is handled by our email provider.

These help pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking pixels added by us. They are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection and request information, such as an IP address, to operate and secure its service under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your choices, retention and removal

  • Local progress: stays with the app's installation. Device backups may also include it, depending on your Apple backup settings. Deleting the app removes its local installation data; offloading it or removing its Home Screen icon is different. Separate backups and cloud copies are not removed by deleting the app.
  • Learning restart: a grown-up can start with easier games while keeping the earned garden, or separately choose to restart the garden too. This changes game progress; it is not a request to erase all saved records or recovery copies. Purchases and comfort settings remain separate.
  • iCloud: turning sync off stops future syncing from that device and keeps both local progress and the last cloud copy. It does not delete either. Recovery copies and earlier save records may remain. Use Apple's available iCloud storage controls to manage data held in your account; Apple explains iCloud storage and backups. Contact us if you need help finding the relevant controls. The app does not currently offer a one-tap cloud-data deletion feature.
  • Support: messages may be kept while handling your request and maintaining necessary support records. You can ask us to delete a support conversation; applicable recordkeeping obligations may affect what can be removed.

Children and privacy

Letterpuff is designed for children to play with a grown-up's involvement. It has no chat, public profile, advertising or analytics SDK. A grown-up check protects access to settings and purchase controls from casual taps. Parents can contact us with privacy questions or if personal information was sent to support by mistake.

Policy updates and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when Letterpuff's practices change. For privacy questions or support-message deletion requests, contact onelldev at onellromandev@gmail.com.
