Privacy, explained simply.

Letterpuff is a learning game provided by onelldev. There are no ads or separate Letterpuff accounts. We do not use learning analytics to track how your child plays. Purchase and backup behavior differs by platform, as explained below.

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Progress saves on your device. Supported iOS versions offer optional private iCloud saves; Android offers device-managed backup and transfer. Apple or Google Play handles purchases on its platform. If you email support, we receive the message you choose to send.

Information on your device

Letterpuff stores learning progress, completed activities, difficulty, earned rewards, garden placements and feeding history locally. It also stores preferences such as speech, haptics and reduced animation. This information lets the game remember where you left off.

The app does not ask for a child's name, date of birth, email address, photos, contacts or location. It does not record the microphone. Spoken prompts use audio and speech processing on the device. We do not run a Letterpuff server that receives gameplay progress.

iPhone and iPad: optional private iCloud saves

In versions where iCloud saves are available, a grown-up can turn them on in the Grown-up Corner. The app then sends learning progress, earned rewards, garden changes, feeding records and save identifiers to a private CloudKit database associated with the device's iCloud Apple Account. Save identifiers help combine changes and avoid duplicate rewards. These saves are not public and are not shared with other players.

Both devices must use the same iCloud Apple Account to share progress. Family Sharing alone does not share a save between different accounts. Comfort settings and an unfinished Letter Fit round stay on each device; purchases are handled separately through Apple. The app keeps a local iCloud account reference to detect account changes and avoid mixing saves.

Local saving continues offline. Sync depends on iCloud availability, connectivity and account storage. Apple operates iCloud and processes account and service information under Apple's Privacy Policy. Availability of iCloud saves can vary by app version.

Purchases through Apple

Letters, Letter Fit, Numbers and My Garden are free. Optional Premium is a one-time purchase for six additional games and Play Remix, with no subscription. Apple processes payment and restoration through StoreKit. Letterpuff reads verified transaction and purchase-status information to provide access. We do not receive your payment-card details, and purchase ownership is not stored in the gameplay cloud save.

Apple may provide the developer with App Store sales and transaction reports under its own service policies. Letterpuff does not add advertising or analytics tracking to purchases.

Android: device backup and transfer

Android can include committed learning progress, your garden, saved rounds and comfort settings in device backup or transfer. On supported Google devices, backup uses the Google Account selected in Android settings. Letterpuff does not sign you in or read that account’s identity, and we do not receive gameplay saves on a Letterpuff server.

Reinstalling Letterpuff can recover your progress from a completed Android backup. On supported Google devices, use the same Google Account and enable device backup before removing the app; Android also needs an eligible saved copy available to restore. Android restores that copy during installation or device setup, before Letterpuff opens. A backup is not created immediately after every move, so recent play may be missing. Letterpuff cannot confirm that your Google backup exists or is current. Do not uninstall or clear storage to troubleshoot missing progress without checking your backup first.

On another Android device, choose the previous device’s backup when Android offers it during setup. This is recovery from a saved copy, not live synchronization between active devices. There is no in-app restore-now control, and iPhone saves are not transferred to Android.

Google documents encryption in transit and at rest for its standard Android Backup system. Supported versions with a screen lock add end-to-end protection. Letterpuff requires that additional platform capability for cloud backup on Android 12 and later; its older-version rules do not require it. We do not promise end-to-end protection on every supported device. Direct device transfer is separate, with availability and protection determined by the transfer service. Google explains Android backup controls.

Only committed gameplay-save files and their recovery copy are included in Letterpuff’s backup rules. Purchase ownership, the billing-setup reminder, SDK databases, temporary files and speech assets are excluded. Backup copies are separate from the local installation; deleting the app does not necessarily delete those copies. Use the backup provider’s available controls for them.

Google’s backup service also processes account identifiers to associate a backup with its owner, and crash logs and diagnostics for troubleshooting and analytics. This is Google’s device-backup service behavior; Letterpuff does not read your Google Account identity or add gameplay analytics. See Google’s backup data explanation.

Google says device backups are deleted when backup is turned off or after 57 days of device inactivity; Google Photos is separate. These are device-wide controls, not a Letterpuff-only delete button. Read the provider’s confirmation carefully before deleting a backup, because other apps’ recovery data may also be removed. See Google’s backup retention and deletion guidance.

Android: Google Play purchases

Letters, Letter Fit, Number Hunt and My Garden are free. Optional Full Unlock adds six games and Play Remix through one purchase, with no subscription. Google Play displays the regional price and handles checkout and restoration. Letterpuff receives product and purchase-status information, verifies purchases and sends the purchase token to Google Play to acknowledge a completed purchase. The app does not receive payment-card details or attach learning progress or garden contents to purchase requests.

After passing the parent gate and entering the Grown-up Corner for the first time, Google Play purchase checks start even if you only change sound or comfort settings. Letterpuff stores a private reminder that setup was started; it contains no purchase token or ownership and is excluded from gameplay backup. After that, checks can run when the app returns to the foreground, including on play screens. New purchases and manual Restore Purchases actions remain in the grown-up area. Leaving the area does not necessarily stop an already-running check. The grown-up question is not identity verification or proof of parental consent.

Google Play receives billing-request metadata, including the app identifier, billing-library version, a billing-session identifier and purchase information used for acknowledgement. Letterpuff does not send learning or garden saves with those requests and does not operate a payment-data server. Google handles its account, payment and service information under its own practices and may provide transaction information to the developer. See Google’s Privacy Policy and retention explanation.

Restore Purchases uses the Google Play account that bought Full Unlock, separately from gameplay recovery, and may need connectivity. If ownership is revoked, premium play becomes unavailable while learning progress remains saved. Clearing Letterpuff’s storage does not delete Google’s transaction records. Letterpuff support cannot promise to delete records held by Google.

Android: spoken prompts

Prompts use bundled recordings and on-device speech processing. No microphone is recorded and prompts are not streamed to a developer server. An Android fallback requires a suitable installed offline English voice. Speech and music can be turned off in the Grown-up Corner.

Support messages and this website

Please avoid sending a child's personal details, passwords, payment-card information or private Apple or Google Account information. Support email is handled by our email provider.

These help pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking pixels added by us. They are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection and request information, such as an IP address, to operate and secure its service under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your choices, retention and removal

  • Local progress: stays with the app's installation. Device backups may also include it, depending on your platform and backup settings. Deleting the app removes its local installation data; offloading it or removing its Home Screen icon is different. Separate backups and cloud copies are not necessarily removed by deleting the app.
  • Learning restart: a grown-up can start with easier games while keeping the earned garden, or separately choose to restart the garden too. This changes game progress; it is not a request to erase all saved records or recovery copies. Purchases and comfort settings remain separate.
  • iCloud: turning sync off stops future syncing from that device and keeps both local progress and the last cloud copy. It does not delete either. Recovery copies and earlier save records may remain. Use Apple's available iCloud storage controls to manage data held in your account; Apple explains iCloud storage and backups. Contact us if you need help finding the relevant controls. The app does not currently offer a one-tap cloud-data deletion feature.
  • Support: messages may be kept while handling your request and maintaining necessary support records. You can ask us to delete a support conversation; applicable recordkeeping obligations may affect what can be removed.

Children and privacy

Letterpuff is designed for children to play with a grown-up's involvement. It has no chat, public profile or advertising, and we do not use learning analytics. A grown-up check protects access to settings and purchase controls from casual taps. Parents can contact us with privacy questions or if personal information was sent to support by mistake.

Policy updates and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when Letterpuff's practices change. For privacy questions or support-message deletion requests, contact onelldev at onellromandev@gmail.com.
